Mappr keys v__VERSION__

Everything is reachable from the keyboard. Your hands never leave home row.

Create

⏎A new node beside this one, same level, same branch. Type, Enter, type, Enter lays down a whole level without stopping.
⇧⏎A new node at the same level but in the next branch along, so you can fill a level across the whole map without walking back to each parent. It goes in the order you see: top to bottom on a sideways branch, left to right on an up or down one, and wraps round on the same side.
⌘⏎A child, one level deeper.
⌘←↑→↓From the centre: start a branch that way. On a branch: the same direction again goes deeper, a sideways direction adds a sibling on that side.
hold Tab + ←↑→↓With a selection up, the arrow back toward the centre groups it: a new parent slides in above every selected branch, wherever they were, and they all become its children, in map order. Type its name. Otherwise: insert a node in between. Point back toward the centre and the new node becomes this one's parent. Point outward and it slides in above this node's children, adopting them. This is how you backtrack and add a layer you did not plan for.

Move

←↑→↓Walk the tree. Past the last of a node's siblings the arrow carries on into the next branch along at the same level, staying on that side of the map. Unlike Tab it does not wrap: run out of map that way and it stops.
Arrows while typingStop typing and start moving, in one press. ⌥+arrow moves the cursor inside the text instead.
Tab / ⇧TabCycle every node at the same level. On a focused branch's own node, it moves the focus round the sibling branches instead, like the arrows do, and wraps.
⌘KJump to any node by typing a few letters of it. Works across the whole map, opens folded branches and steps out of focus to get there.
⌘0Zoom out to the whole map. Steps out of focus first, so this always shows you everything.
⇧⌘0Fit just the selected branch, or the selected frame.
⌘.Take me to the selection: centre on it at 100%. Esc afterwards puts the camera back where it was. Leaving a focus does the same: you land at the zoom you had before you went in.
⌘/Focus: the selected branch fills the screen. Press it again to go back to exactly the view you had, zoom and all. Esc steps out one level at a time.
Arrows while focusedOn the focused node itself, a sideways arrow carries the focus to the branch beside it and the arrow pointing back toward the centre lifts it a level, so you can walk a large map branch by branch without stepping out and back in. Outward still goes to the first child.

Edit

Space or any letterRetype the selected node, replacing its text. A new node is ready the same way: it is selected rather than opened, so there is no cursor to look at, and your first letter starts it. Leave it blank and it goes. Cursor in new nodes, in the style panel, opens new nodes for typing instead.
⌥⏎Line break inside a node.
EscStop typing. A node left blank is dropped.
⌫Delete just the node. Its children move up and take its place, and flash so you can see where they went. With a selection up it deletes exactly what is selected, and anything underneath that you did not select moves up the same way.
⌘⌫Delete the whole branch, or every selected branch. ⌘Z brings any delete back.
⌘Z / ⌘⇧ZUndo / redo. Retyping a node is a step of its own, so overwriting one by accident comes back with ⌘Z, even while you are still typing.
⌥←↑→↓Move the node itself. Sideways reorders it among its siblings, and past the last one carries it on into the next branch along on that side, outward tucks it under the sibling before it, back toward the centre promotes it a level. The whole branch travels with it. With a selection up, every selected branch moves together and stays selected, so you can keep pressing.
⌘EFold or unfold a branch. A folded node wears a badge with the number hidden underneath; click the badge to open it. With a selection up it folds every selected node at once, so a whole level goes away in one press.
⌥JMerge the selected nodes into one. The first in map order keeps its place; its text becomes all of theirs, one per line, and it takes every child they had. Links and frames follow it. Also on the selection bar.
⌥S / ⌥⇧SSplit a node with several lines into one node per line: as siblings right after it, or with ⇧ as its children. It keeps the first line and its own children, and leading bullets or numbers are dropped. Works on every selected node at once.
⌥OSort. A small menu, a number picks: A to Z, Z to A, reverse, biggest branch first, done last, or back to how it was. It sorts the selected siblings among the places they already hold, or with one node selected, that node's children. On the centre each side is sorted on its own.
⌥XCarry. Picks up the selection (or the selected node): it goes faint where it is, and the arrows, or a click, choose where it goes. ⏎ drops it inside the ringed node, ⇧⏎ right after it, Esc puts it back. Nothing changes until the drop, and one ⌘Z undoes it.
⌘DDuplicate the branch, right below itself. With a selection up, every selected branch is duplicated and the copies come out selected.
⌘⇧D / ⌘⇧FMark done (struck through) / flag (a small pennant).

Select and frames

⇧←↑→↓Grow the selection node by node, the way Shift grows a text selection. Stepping back lets go of the last one. While typing, it selects text as usual.
⌘⇧←↑→↓Select whole branches that way. Outward: this node's branch. Sideways: this node and every sibling that way, with their branches. Back toward the centre: the parent's whole branch. From the centre: every branch on that side.
⇧ + dragLasso part of the map. Select a node and its whole branch comes with it. The Select button makes plain dragging do this too.
⇧ + clickSelect a node's whole sibling row: every child of its parent, in one gesture. This is how you get a level in hand to fold or delete it.
⌘ASelect the selected node's whole level, across the map. Press it again for everything on screen. While anything is selected, the top bar shows how many, with Copy, Duplicate and Delete buttons.
⌘GWrap the selection in a frame and name it. Frames resize themselves as the map underneath grows.
⌘⇧GUngroup the selected frame.
⌘CCopy as an indented bullet list: exactly what is selected, each node under its nearest selected parent. With nothing selected, the selected node's branch (on the centre, the whole map), or the selected frame. Copies made here also keep done and flag marks, folds, and links between the copied nodes.
⌘XCut: copy, then delete what was copied.
⌘V / ⇧⌘VPaste as children of the selected node, or as siblings right after it. The pasted nodes come in selected, and point the way their new parent does. Pasting onto a blank node you just made puts the paste in its place, and one undo takes both back.
⇧⌘CCopy as an image, ready to paste into a deck or a doc.
Double-click a frame nameRename it.
Frames in viewsA frame follows what is on screen: in a focus it wraps the part you can see, while presenting it grows as its nodes are revealed, and in a network, where the tree is set aside, frames step out of the way.

Links

⌘ + clickTie the selected node to the one you click, anywhere on the map. A faint arc joins them. The same pair again unties. A link says these two relate; it does not change who owns whom, so nothing moves.
⌘⇧ + clickTie two branches. Ties the selected node to the one you click as a branch tie: one heavier arc with a bracket at each end, and in connections everything under either node counts as the same network, so a whole branch relates to a whole branch without a tie per node. The same again removes it. From the keyboard, select the two nodes and press ⌥⇧T.
The ∿ badgeHow many links land on this node. Click it to walk to the other end, which is usually off screen; clicking again cycles through the rest. A folded branch carries the badge for everything tied up inside it, and walking there opens it.
A hollow endThe node you actually tied is folded away inside the one the arc reaches. Better than a line running into nothing.
⌘1 or ⌥1Normal view. The number keys are views, and 1 is the plain map. From any other view it puts you back where you were, focus and all. The ⌥ versions are there because a browser tab may keep ⌘ plus a number for itself.
⌘2 or ⌥2 / ⌥LDim to the links. The whole map stays put and everything no link touches drops to a ghost, so you keep your bearings while the arcs come forward. Pressed while focused on a tied branch, it goes straight to that node's network. Press it again, or Esc, to turn it off: you land back where you were, focus and all.
⇧ + double-click in connectionsClear every link on that node. ⌘Z brings them back.
Tab / arrows in connectionsStep from one network to the next. While dimmed, they move the selection network to network; in a focused network, they do it when the network's centre is selected, and move within the network otherwise. Tab wraps round, arrows stop at the ends.
⌘/ with a lens upOn a tied node, Focus shows its whole network: the node, everything tied to it, and everything tied to those, and nothing else. It is laid out by the links rather than by the tree, so a chain reads as a chain; nothing is moved for good. ⌘/ again, or Esc, steps back to where you opened it from. While dimmed, Focus on an untied node does what it always did.
Moving in a networkThe tree is hidden, so the keys follow what is on screen: arrows go to the nearest node that way, Tab walks the network nearest first, and anything you make with Enter or ⌘+arrow is tied to the node you made it from, so it stays in view. Moving nodes and inserting between them are off until you leave.
⌘E in a networkFolds the network instead of the tree: everything reached only through the selected node goes, and it wears a badge counting them. Click the badge or press it again to bring them back.
⌘C / ⇧⌘CIn a network: copy the network as an outline (the centre, what is tied to it, what is tied to those) or as an image of just the network. The Copy menu in the bar does both.

Present

⌘3 or ⌥3Present. Walks the map (or the focused branch) one node at a time in reading order, showing nothing you have not reached yet. Each node lands where it will stay and the camera follows. → or Space next, ⇧→ the whole level at once, ← back, O a look at everything so far, Home from the top. ⇧O shows the whole story at once. After the last node, one more step lights the whole story and stands back. ⌘K jumps to any node in the story, telling everything before it. A click moves on too, and a right-click goes back. Leave and come back and it picks up where you were. The bar has a full screen button. Esc, ⌘1 or ⌘3 ends it and puts you back where you were. Folded branches stay folded.
R while presentingArrange the story. The whole walk comes up, numbered. ⌘+click a sibling to say this, then that, or from a parent to one of its children to say which child that level opens with; the same pair again undoes. From the keyboard, Space picks a node up and Space on another puts the picked one before it. ⌫ unpicks a node and resets every arrangement underneath it; unlinking a node does the same. Order only ever changes things inside one level. A level you never touch is told in map order; once you pick any of its children, the ones you did not pick are skipped, and arrange mode marks them so nothing goes missing by accident. R again presents the new order from the top. It is saved with the map, and a map can hold several stories: the story menu in the bar switches between them (or [ and ], or a number while it is open), adds, renames and deletes them. Delete asks twice, and ⌘Z brings one back.
⌘C / ⇧⌘CWhile presenting: copy the story so far as an outline or an image, mid-talk. From the overview of the whole story, the finale or arrange mode, they copy the whole story. The Copy menu in the bar has all four, and a number picks while it is open. Images carry the story's frames and leave out what the story skips.

Maps and files

⌘VPaste an indented list and it becomes a branch. Tabs, spaces, bullets, numbers and markdown headings all read as depth. Paste onto the centre and the top level balances left and right.
Export ▾PNG and SVG of the frame or the whole map, and the full JSON with every setting.
Maps ▾Every map you have made, newest first. A map is named after its centre node, so renaming the centre renames the map. Copy and delete live in the same row.
⌥NA new map.
⌥BBreak out a branch into a map of its own. The branch moves, with its marks, folds, frames and the links inside it, and a link takes its place. Links that crossed the cut are removed and counted. On a link, ⌥B brings the map back in as a branch; the map goes to Recently deleted unless something else links to it. ⌘Z undoes either, map and all.
⌥KLink the selected node to a map: pick one, or make a new one named after the node. A link shows the map's name and size, and its look is in the style panel under Map links. Its name follows the map, so open the map to rename it.
⌥⏎ / double-clickOpen a linked map. The top bar keeps a trail of the maps you came through; ⌥U or a name in the trail goes back up, to the link you left from. Linked from in the top bar says which other maps link here. A link whose map was deleted says so, and ⌥⏎ restores the map.
⌥MSwitch map: pinned maps first, then recent ones. Type to filter, arrows and ⏎ to open. It starts on the map you were on before, so ⌥M ⏎ swaps back and forth.
⌥PPin or unpin the open map (in the switcher, the highlighted one). Pinned maps sit at the top of the switcher and of Maps ▾, and the pin shows next to the Mappr name.
⌥⇧1…9Open a pinned map, in pin order.
⌥TGive the map a colour tag; press again for the next colour, and past the last one it clears. The dot beside each map in the lists does the same on a click.
⌥`Back to the map you were on before.
MapsEvery map is stored on its own. Delete asks once before it goes, and the last few deleted maps wait under Recently deleted in the same panel with a Restore button.
TouchOne finger pans, two fingers pinch to zoom, and a tap opens a folded branch. Making a map needs a keyboard, so touch is for reading one you already have.

Canvas

The cameraFollows on its own. It holds still while you work mid-screen and slides only when you are about to run off an edge.
Drag / scrollPan. ⌘+scroll zooms.
⌘,Style panel. It never takes the keyboard, so shortcuts keep working while it is open.
